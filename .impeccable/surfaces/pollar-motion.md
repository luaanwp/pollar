## Mode and authority

Operate. User explicitly requested richer motion from four supplied references,
integrated into the current redesign rather than replacing the visual world.
The newer request authorizes digit rolling and a finite goal celebration;
financial calculations, typography, layout hierarchy and privacy remain binding.

## Motion thesis

Actual balances change visibly; a credit card expands into its real statement;
its reverse exposes existing terms, never fabricated bank details. Real chart
geometry morphs while exact textual values stay authoritative. Completion
feedback follows saved goal state. Routine feedback remains short and clickable.

## Budget and boundaries

140/240/360/700ms roles, bounded 20ms stagger, restrained tilt and 0.98 press.
No infinite loops, input blocking or persistence waits. Reduced motion cuts
own effects. Offstage branch tickers stop. Privacy destroys outgoing numeric
painting and chart geometry. The preview is labelled, isolated and local.
