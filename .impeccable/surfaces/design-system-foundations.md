## Scope and mode

Operate: phase 1 of the user-pinned UI_UX_REDESIGN_POLLAR.md. This is an explicit
component/catalog extension inside the existing brand, not a concept workshop.

## Job and direction

Developers inspect outlined, borderless and financial surface roles, opening
amount hierarchy, keyboard focus and privacy in light/dark and narrow layouts.
Demonstrative amounts are labelled; the isolated catalog initializes no auth
or database and never creates financial records.

## Constraints and boundaries

Keep existing defaults and production routes. Solid focal surface only in this
phase; gradient proposals wait for derived tokens and contrast evidence.
Privacy must preserve geometry and hide accessible amounts. Money formatting
and financial rules remain authoritative and unchanged.

## Unresolved

Shell, overview and other product compositions are later phases. No chart,
assistant, insight engine or Open Finance functionality is introduced here.
